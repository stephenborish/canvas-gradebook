Canvas Gradebook+ - Hidden Gems: third-party code and assets
=============================================================

The three Hidden Gems effects (src/gems/*.js) are adapted from the open-source
projects below, and use the textures in this folder. Each entry says what was
used, where it lives here, and under what terms. Full licence texts follow.

SNOW (src/gems/snow.js)
  - react-cinematic-snow, Khoa Ma, https://github.com/khoama/react-cinematic-snow
    Used: components/WebGLRenderer.ts (shaders) and components/Snowfall.tsx
    (layers, particle model, dynamic wind), ported from React/TypeScript.
    MIT licence, below.
  - snowfall-canvas, Maxim (ux-ui-pro), https://github.com/ux-ui-pro/snowfall-canvas
    Used: performance techniques of src/index.ts (typed-array particles,
    capped pixel density, density by area, adaptive particle reduction).
    MIT licence, below.

AUTUMN LEAVES (src/gems/leaves.js, leaves.webp)
  - falling-animation, phongdh, https://github.com/phong-baruby/falling-package
    Used: src/Particle.ts, src/animations/index.ts, the spawn loop of
    src/FallingAnimation.ts and drawParticle of src/CanvasRenderer.ts.
    MIT licence, below.
  - leaves.webp: individual leaves cut from ambientCG's LeafSet006/007/011/
    012/015/021/027/028/030 atlases, https://ambientcg.com. CC0 1.0 (public
    domain dedication): "All assets on ambientCG are provided under the
    Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal License."
  - Idea only, no code: the pointer pushing leaves aside, as in Autumn
    Playground by YiYue (Joanna) Qiao, https://github.com/YIYUE-Joanna/autumn-playground
    (that repository has no licence, so none of its code is used).

CHRISTMAS LIGHTS (src/gems/lights.js, lights-bulbs-96.png)
  - "Christmas Light Smashfest" (XLSF), part of Snowstorm by Scott Schiller,
    https://github.com/scottschiller/Snowstorm (lights/)
    Used: lights/christmaslights.js (layout, sprite offsets, rotateLights and
    randomLights sequences) and lights/image/bulbs-96x96-top.png
    (here lights-bulbs-96.png). BSD licence, below.
