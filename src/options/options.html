Comment Snippets

Speed up grading with instant feedback templates in SpeedGrader and the Gradebook.

How Comment Snippets Work

  1. Choose a shortcut name: Enter a short keyword (e.g. late, PDF, evidence). The / is optional – both work automatically.
  2. Write your feedback template: Enter your comment message below. Supports multiple sentences and paragraphs (up to 2,000 characters).
  3. Save your changes: Click the blue Save changes button at the bottom-left to save your snippets to Chrome.
  4. Expand while grading: In SpeedGrader, Grade Detail Tray, or Gradebook comments, type / followed by your shortcut (e.g. /late), then press the Tab key.
Helpful Tip: Pressing Tab instantly replaces your shortcut with the full comment in place. Shortcuts are case-insensitive (/hw and /HW both match). Focus never leaves your comment box.

Your Snippet Templates

0 of 100 snippets

In bulk mode, separate each snippet with a blank line. The first line is the trigger name, and following lines are the comment text. Up to 100 snippets of 2,000 characters each; anything beyond that is pointed out on Save rather than saved.

Class Schedule

Connect the calendar that holds your classes, and every Canvas due date box shows that day’s schedule – click a class to use its time.

Step 1 · Copy your class calendar’s link from Google Calendar

Open Google Calendar settings ↗
  1. In Google Calendar, click the gear at the top right, then Settings (or use the button above).
  2. In the list on the left, click the name of the calendar that holds your classes. A calendar your school shares with you (for example All Classes) is under Settings for other calendars; one you made yourself is under Settings for my calendars.
  3. Under that calendar’s name, click Integrate calendar.
  4. Find the box labelled Public URL to this calendar. Click in it, select everything in it (Ctrl+A, or ⌘+A on a Mac) and copy it (Ctrl+C / ⌘+C).
It looks like this in Google Calendar:
Public URL to this calendar https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=…

Preview

Then, in Canvas

  1. Open a date or time box: click any Due, Available from or Until box when you create or edit an assignment. That day’s classes appear beside it.
  2. Click a class: its start time goes into the time box (the small End button uses its end time). Use the arrows to see another day; picking a class there puts that day in the date box too.
If Connect says the calendar is not public: Google only shares a calendar’s classes through that link when the calendar is public. For a calendar you made, open Access permissions for events in the same settings page, tick Make available to public and choose See all event details – or, instead, paste its Secret address in iCal format (in Integrate calendar), which works without making it public. For a calendar your school shares with you, ask whoever runs it to make it public. The Public address in iCal format works too.
Good to know: the calendar is read again every few hours (or with Refresh in the schedule panel). Classes named like the Canvas course you are editing are marked This course. The calendar’s link travels with your settings through Chrome sync (never into your Canvas account); on another computer, click Connect once there to allow it.
