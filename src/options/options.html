Comment snippets

In SpeedGrader or the gradebook comment popover, type /trigger then press Tab. One snippet per block: first line is the trigger, the rest is the comment.

Example:

evidence
Good claim - now tie it back to the evidence in your data.

units
Check your units and significant figures.
