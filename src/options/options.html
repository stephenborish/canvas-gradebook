Comment Snippets

Type a short code to insert a comment you use often.

How it works

  1. Pick a short name: for example late or evidence (no / needed).
  2. Write the comment: it can be several sentences or paragraphs (up to 2,000 characters).
  3. Save: click Save changes at the bottom left.
  4. Use it: in any comment box in SpeedGrader or the gradebook, type /late and press Tab.
Tip: capital letters don't matter: /hw and /HW both work.

Class Schedule

Connect your class calendar, and Canvas's date boxes show that day's classes. Click a class to use its time.

Connect it for me Gradebook+ opens Google Calendar and connects your All Classes calendar. Sign in to your school Google account first. When Chrome asks, click Allow.

Canvas’s date boxes stay as they are. You can connect a calendar any time.

I already have a calendar link
Where do I find my link?

Google Calendar – your “All Classes” calendar

That button opens Google Calendar and highlights the link. Or by hand:

  1. Open Google Calendar settings (or click the gear at the top right, then Settings).
  2. On the left, under Settings for other calendars, click All Classes.
  3. Copy the whole link in the URL box (it starts with webcal://) and paste it above.

Classes in another calendar? Click that calendar, then Integrate calendar, and copy its Secret address in iCal format (keep it private, like a password). Links from your school, Outlook or Apple Calendar work too.

Preview

Then, in Canvas

  1. Click a date or time box: any Due, Available from or Until box. That day’s classes appear next to it.
  2. Click a class: its start time fills the box (the small End button uses its end time). Use the arrows to pick a class on another day.
Good to know: the calendar updates every few hours, or click Refresh in the schedule panel. On another computer, click Allow calendar access on this computer here once.
